# Prometheus domain glossary

**User:** A person whose identity and session have been authenticated by the sign-in system. Being a User alone does not grant access to Prometheus business data.

**Member:** A person admitted to the Prometheus workspace. A Member has an independently managed membership state; only an active Member may use workspace features.

**Project Lead:** The Member designated to lead a specific Project. Workspace administrator privileges and creation of a Project do not automatically confer this role.

**Project Editor:** A Member granted permission to change a Project through its membership rules, such as the Project Lead or a Project Member with CAN_EDIT access.
